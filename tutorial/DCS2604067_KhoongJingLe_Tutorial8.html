More About Faker

Lee "Faker" Sang-hyeok, born May 7, 1996, in Seoul, South Korea, is widely recognized as the greatest player in League of Legends history. He made his professional debut in 2013 with SK Telecom T1 (now T1) as a prodigy mid-laner, instantly captivating global audiences with his mechanical skill and earned the nickname "The Unkillable Demon King." Over more than a decade at the highest tier of esports, Faker has driven T1 to five World Championship titles—winning in 2013, 2015, 2016, 2023, and 2024—alongside two Mid-Season Invitational trophies and ten LCK regional titles. Beyond his mechanical precision, his longevity, adaptability, and leadership transformed him into a global cultural icon, culminating in his induction as the inaugural member of Riot Games' Hall of Legends in 2024.
