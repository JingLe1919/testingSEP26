Short Story: The Sheperd Boy and the Wolf


A shepherd boy in a village used to take his herd of sheep across the fields near the forest. He felt this job was very dull and wanted to have some fun.


One day whule grazing the sheep, he shouted, "Wolf! Wolf! The wolf is carrying away a lamb!" Farmers working in the nearby fields came running for help but didn't find any wolf.


The boy laughted and replied, "It was just fun. There is no wolf here". The boy played a similar trick repeatedly for many days.


After some days while the sheperd boy was in the field with the herd of sheep, suddenly, a wolf came out from the nearby forest and attacked one of the lambs. The boy was frightned and cried loudly, "Wolf! Wolf! The wold is carring a lamb away!"

The farmers thought the boy was playing mischeif again. So, no one paid attention to him and didn't come to his help


Moral: No one believes a liar even if they speak about the truth once.
